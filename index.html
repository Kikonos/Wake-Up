Details

Upload your My Roster PDF. The app reads it on your phone, finds every flight leaving DXB and sets each e-gate 2h30 before departure. Check them against your roster the first few times. The first upload needs an internet connection.

Typing: 4 digits, e.g. 1430. A short 615 reads as 06:15. The date moves on a day after each add.

Alarms: tap the clock next to the wake-up time or the leave-home time to add that alarm through your "E-Gate Alarm" shortcut. Clock alarms have no date, so add them within 24 hours of waking up. Tap again to mark as not added.

All times are Dubai time (GMT+4). Duties disappear 1 hour after e-gate.
